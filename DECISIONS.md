# Decisions

Settled choices for the league site, newest first. Anything not listed here is still open; see `docs/DESIGN.md` section 12.

## For the commissioner's review

The owner asked whether a player could take advantage of the setup, then asked for fixes (Sept 28), to be reviewed by the commissioner later. Each change is a setting the commissioner can change for a future week on the Admin page's Rules editor, except where noted. The Rules page explains each one to members.

| Change | Why | Before |
|---|---|---|
| **Teaser prices for 4–10 legs:** each leg past 3 needs 0.35 percentage points more to break even than a leg on Splash's 3-leg card, rounded down. At 6 points: 4 legs +285, 5 +425, 6 +615, 7 +860, 8 +1150, 9 +1550, 10 +2100. The 2- and 3-leg rows (Splash's) are unchanged. Full table in `docs/DESIGN.md` section 3. | A flat break-even multiplied any per-leg edge: a player whose 6-point legs win 75% of the time expected about +72% on a 10-leg card. Now it's about +24%, close to the 3-leg card's +18%. | 4–10 legs kept the 3-leg card's break-even (10 legs +2950 at 6 points). |
| **No two legs from the same game** in a parlay or a teaser. | Legs from one game tend to win or lose together (a big favorite covering and the over), and multiplying their odds pays them as if they didn't. | Parlays could combine a spread or moneyline with the total; teasers could combine anything. |
| **No betting both sides of a game:** once an entry has a bet on one side, it can't bet the other team (by spread or moneyline, in any mix), or the other side of the total, in a separate bet. The database enforces it. Only bets the member can see count, so a co-manager added mid-week isn't told about hidden bets by being refused. | Betting both sides met the 30% minimum for the cost of the vig, about 1.4% of the bank a week, instead of risking anything. | Allowed. |
| **Undo only while the line is unchanged:** the 5-minute window stays, but a bet can't be undone once one of its lines has moved (or come off the board). Every undo is checked against fresh lines: the database refuses it unless the board's lines came from a pull that went out after the undo was asked for. Undo pulls have limits of their own (10 a day per member, 100 in all, one per member every 10 seconds), so undoing can't use up the refreshes bets rely on. If the lines can't be pulled (the feed is down, a limit is used up, the odds credits are nearly gone), the bet stays. Undo goes only through the site's bet service. A teaser's legs count only by their number, since the table pays it. Still open: a move shows only once the sportsbook has moved and the odds feed has picked it up, so an undo asked for right after news can still go through. A shorter window narrows that; no undo at all closes it. | Undo was a free option on news: bet just before an announcement, and undo whatever the news hurt. | Any bet could be undone within 5 minutes, before kickoff. |
| **Lines pulled every 10 minutes in the 3 hours before a kickoff** (every 30 otherwise), and a member's bets can trigger a line refresh every 5 minutes instead of every 10. League settings, not rules: `pull_near_kickoff_minutes`, `near_kickoff_hours`, `bet_refresh_member_minutes`. | Lines move most right before kickoff (inactives, weather), and a member who couldn't trigger a refresh bet on whatever the site last pulled, up to 30 minutes old. Costs roughly 800 more credits a month (about 15 hours a week are within 3 hours of a kickoff; 4 more pulls an hour at 3 credits each), well inside the plan's 100,000. | Every 30 minutes; one refresh per member every 10 minutes. |
| **Fair-play flag:** the Admin page's Recent problems lists any two entries that shared a manager when their bets were placed (or whose bets the same member placed) and took opposite sides of a game, once both picks are public. Not blocked. | Someone with two entries could bet them against each other so one of them is sure to climb. Blocking it would tell one entry about another's hidden pick. The Rules page says such entries shouldn't bet against each other; what happens when one does is for the commissioner. One step further would be possible: block it when the same member places both bets, which reveals nothing. | Nothing. |

## Kept as defaults

Defaults chosen while fixing the review findings (Sept 28–29), adding player props (Oct 5) and the props safeguards (Oct 6). On 2026-10-07 the owner went through the ones members notice (now under Settled) and kept these as they are. Each is built and tested; say the word to change any of them.

| Default | Why |
|---|---|
| A co-manager added mid-week sees the entry's hidden bets placed before they joined only at kickoff, like everyone else. | Otherwise an admin could add themselves to any entry and see its picks. |
| A bet can't be undone once its week has been closed, even inside the 5 minutes. | An early close could otherwise be used to dodge the 30% minimum. |
| The season's last week is closed by an admin with its own "Close the season" step; "Open next week" won't close a week until the next week's games are on the board. | The site can't tell "the season is over" from "next week's games aren't posted yet", and betting should never stall with no week open. |
| Once a week is open, an admin can move a kickoff later but not earlier; real schedule changes come from the feed, and "postponed" stops betting on a game at once. | Moving a kickoff earlier would show its picks early. |
| Entering a game's same final score again regrades its bets. | A way to fix a bet graded wrong without changing the score. |
| A new rules version can't start before one that's already scheduled. | Keeps "the newest version applies from its week on" unambiguous. |
| Standings' "This week" and "Last week" count bets by their week, not by when they were graded. | A Monday-night bet graded after midnight belongs to its week. |
| New members must be given a display name; it's never taken from their email. | Display names are public. |
| Bets can trigger line refreshes at most once every 2 minutes overall, once every 5 minutes (10 before the fairness review) and 20 times a day per member, and 200 times a day in all; past that, bets use the scheduled pulls' lines. Undos have their own limits: 10 pulls a day per member, 100 in all, at most one per member every 10 seconds. | Caps the credits any member (or script) can spend at about 60 a day on bets and 30 on undos, and everyone together at about 600 and 300. The limits are league settings. |
| An admin can't make themselves a manager of an entry that already has managers or has bets riding; another admin has to. An entry's last manager can't be removed while it has bets riding: add the new manager first. | Being a manager shows an entry's hidden bets from then on. |
| A new start time from the feed moves a kickoff only when two pulls in a row agree (a game due within the hour follows the first reading of an earlier time). A start already in the past is never taken for a game more than an hour away. | One bad reading could otherwise show a game's picks early. |
| When an admin voids a bet in a week that has already closed, it stops counting toward that week's minimum and the minimum is worked out again: the new shortfall, less the part waived at the close for lack of units, which stays waived. More owed is taken only from units free now, and any part that can't be is waived too. | Nobody is charged later for units they didn't have at the close, and the result depends only on which bets count, not on the order corrections come in. A voided losing bet that leaves an entry short nets out: the stake comes back and is charged as shortfall. |
| A prop at a price no book posts for a main line is left off the board (an over/under must carry a 0–20% margin, each side between -300 and +250; an anytime TD between -500 and +2500), and the next book's prop is used. | A feed glitch shouldn't be bettable. |
